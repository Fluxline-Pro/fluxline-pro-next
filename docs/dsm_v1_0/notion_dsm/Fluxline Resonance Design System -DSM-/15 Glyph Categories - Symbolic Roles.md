## 🔧 Integration Ideas

- Use **Shadow Glyph** to tag modules that explore resistance, avoidance, or unconscious patterns—especially in early PT or emotional stewardship work.
- Use **Resonance Glyph** to mark curriculum moments of clarity, alignment, or breakthrough—ideal for integration phases or client feedback rituals.
- Use **Indifference Glyph** to signal emotional dormancy or stagnation—great for tracking metabolic flatlines, hormonal dips, or motivational lulls.
