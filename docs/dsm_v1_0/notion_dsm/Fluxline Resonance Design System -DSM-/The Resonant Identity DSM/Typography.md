# Typography

Excellent — let’s lock in your **typography system** so it matches the visual and tonal identity of *The Resonant Identity* brand. You already have a strong typographic foundation from your earlier work with Gotham, so we’ll build on that rather than reinvent it.

---

### **Primary Typeface**

**Gotham Family**

- **Weights:**Light / Book / Medium / Bold
- **Use:**Headings, subheadings, and key identity statements
- **Tone:**Modern, geometric, confident, architectural — perfectly aligned with your mythic‑technical aesthetic

**Color pairing:**

- Headings in **Teal (RGB 0 180 190 / CMYK 75 0 15 0)**
- Subheadings or secondary text in **White (RGB 255 255 255 / CMYK 0 0 0 0)**
- Optional accent lines or dividers in **Deep Teal (RGB 0 110 120 / CMYK 90 30 40 20)**

---

### **Secondary Typeface**

**Source Serif Pro** *(or Chronicle Text if you prefer a print‑grade serif)*

- **Use:**Body copy, captions, and long‑form text
- **Tone:**Warm, scholarly, and grounded — balances Gotham’s precision with human resonance

**Color pairing:**

- Body text in **Dark Gray (RGB 40 40 40 / CMYK 0 0 0 85)**
- Highlights or pull quotes in **Teal (RGB 0 180 190 / CMYK 75 0 15 0)**

---

### **Typographic Hierarchy**

| **Level** | **Typeface** | **Weight** | **Case** | **Size Ratio** | **Color** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **H1 / Title** | Gotham Bold | Bold | Title Case | 1.0× | Teal |
| **H2 / Subtitle** | Gotham Book | Medium | Sentence Case | 0.7× | White |
| **Body / Paragraph** | Source Serif Pro Regular | Regular | Sentence Case | 0.45× | Dark Gray |
| **Accent / Quote** | Source Serif Pro Italic | Italic | Sentence Case | 0.45× | Teal |

---

### **Stylistic Notes**

- Maintain **ample letter‑spacing** for clarity and rhythm.
- Use **spaced em dashes** as your signature typographic motif (consistent with your book design system).
- Keep **title case for major headings** and **sentence case for subheads** — it supports your tone of grounded clarity.
- Avoid over‑bolding; let color and spacing carry emphasis.
