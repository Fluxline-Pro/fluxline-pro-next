## 2. Informed Consent

**2.1 Nature of the activity.** Personal training and wellness coaching may include physical exercise, strength and conditioning work, stretching, movement assessment, and related coaching activities. You understand that these activities are physically demanding and involve a risk of injury.

**2.2 Not medical care.** Personal training and wellness coaching are not medical care, physical therapy, or a substitute for the advice of a physician. We do not diagnose, treat, or provide medical advice. You are encouraged to consult a qualified health professional before beginning any exercise program, especially if you have a pre-existing medical condition, are pregnant, or have any concern about your ability to safely participate.

**2.3 Your health information.** Your answers in Part 1 of this packet are part of your representations under this Section. You represent that you have disclosed in Part 1, and to us directly, any medical condition, injury, medication, or physical limitation that could affect your ability to safely participate in the Services, and that the information you have given us is accurate and complete. You agree to tell us promptly if anything about your health changes after you sign.

## 3. Assumption of Risk

**3.1 Risks you are accepting.** You understand and voluntarily accept that participating in personal training and wellness coaching carries inherent risks, including but not limited to: muscle strains, sprains, and other soft-tissue injuries; joint injuries; falls; bruising; cardiovascular events, including in rare cases serious or fatal ones; aggravation of a pre-existing condition; and injury caused by equipment, facility conditions, or the actions of other people present.

**3.2 Voluntary participation.** You are participating voluntarily. You may stop any exercise or session at any time, for any reason, including if something feels unsafe or causes you pain.

**3.3 Your responsibility.** You agree to follow instructions, to use equipment as directed, to tell your trainer if something hurts or feels wrong during a session, and to stay within your own physical limits.

## 4. Release and Waiver of Liability

**4.1 What you are releasing. To the fullest extent permitted by law, you release, waive, and discharge Fluxline Resonance Group, LLC, its owners, employees, contractors, and trainers from any and all liability, claims, demands, or causes of action arising out of ordinary negligence in connection with your participation in the Services, including claims for personal injury, illness, or death.**

**4.2 What this does not cover. This release does not apply to, and does not waive, any claim arising from gross negligence, willful misconduct, or any liability that cannot be waived under applicable law. Nothing in this document limits any consumer protection or other legal right that cannot be waived by agreement.**
