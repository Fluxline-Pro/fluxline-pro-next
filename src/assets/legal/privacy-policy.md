## The short version

**We keep as little as we can.** Fluxline does not retain your questions to the RCF AI Companion or its answers. Our AI provider, Microsoft Azure, keeps temporary automated abuse-detection logs (up to 30 days) under its policy (see Section 5.6). We do not store your birthdate, because we never ask for it. We do not train any AI on what you write or ask. We do not sell your information, and our store and the RCF AI Companion show no ads. Anything we keep beyond what is needed to deliver what you bought is something **you turn on**, and you can turn it off. This policy explains the details. Fluxline's own vocabulary is explained in the Glossary of Terms at fluxline.pro/legal.

## 3. What we collect, by service

When you browse our sites we collect technical and usage information: device and browser type, operating system, IP address, pages visited, time on page, clicks, referral source, and general location derived from your IP address. We collect it through cookies and similar tools (see Section 12).

### 3.2 Your Fluxline account

To make an account, we collect your name and email address and the sign-in method you use (Microsoft, Google, or an email one-time code). We store whether you confirmed you are **18 or older** and the date you confirmed it. **We do not collect your birthdate.**

### 3.3 Store and orders

When you buy from the store we keep an order record: what you bought, the amounts, the order number, the date, and your entitlement (what you now have access to). We store the **exact version of the terms you agreed to and the time you agreed** with the order. Payments are handled by our payment processor, which keeps your card details. We do not store your full card number. For personalized digital files, we keep **download records** (which file, when, and how many times) so that we can resolve payment disputes. If you check out as a guest, we collect your name, email, and phone number.

### 3.4 The RCF AI Companion

The Companion needs a Fluxline account. It processes your question to answer it, and it does not keep the question or the answer. What it holds is below.

| Data | Held? | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Question and answer text | **Not retained by Fluxline.** Processed in memory only. Microsoft Azure keeps temporary automated abuse-detection logs (see Section 5.6). | Not kept by Fluxline. Up to 30 days in Microsoft Azure's abuse-detection logs. |
| Reported answer (only if you report one) | Question, answer, and a reference ID, encrypted, with **no link to your account** | 30 days, then deleted |

| Usage counts | Your daily and monthly answer counts, to enforce plan limits | Daily counts 35 days, monthly counts 13 months |

Everything tied to your plan is deleted within 30 days after the plan ends.

Our apps are **local-first**. Your entries, goals, scores, and notes are stored on your device in an encrypted database. They do not go to Fluxline unless you choose a cloud feature. Any sensitive information you give us, in any service line, is treated with extra care and used only for the purpose you gave it.

If you contact us, we keep the messages. If you subscribe to email, we keep your address and preferences. The Resonant Identity Podcast is distributed through podcast hosts and platforms that give us aggregate listening statistics. Social platforms have their own policies.

## 5. AI and your information

**5.1 How a Companion request flows.** When you ask the Companion a question, it follows these steps, in this order:

1. Your account and your 18+ confirmation are checked. An unconfirmed account does not reach any AI service.
2. Free checks run first: a safety check, a relevance check against the book, and a check against reviewed answers written from the book. Some questions are answered without calling a model.
3. We remove personal identifiers we can detect from your question.
4. The scrubbed question is converted into numbers (an "embedding") so we can find matching passages in the book. The index holds the book, not user content.
5. Our governance system approves the request. It receives **no user identifier of any kind**, not even a pseudonymous one. It sees only the scrubbed question, the book passages, and cost labels such as product and plan. Each run is logged only under a generic random ID.
6. A language model writes a short answer using only those passages. A second check runs on every answer.
7. The answer is shown to you. Fluxline stores nothing about the question or answer. Microsoft Azure's temporary abuse-detection logs are described in 5.6.

**5.2 Honest limits of scrubbing.** Our filters catch many personal identifiers, and no filter is perfect. **Please do not type personal, health, or other sensitive information into the Companion.**

**5.3 No training. Ever.** We do not use your questions, answers, entries, or any user content to train, fine-tune, or tune any AI model. When the book is updated, we rebuild the index. We do not train on it. We do not offer an opt-in to contribute questions.

**5.4 Models and where they run.** Cloud AI for the Companion uses models from Microsoft (primary), with backups from other approved providers, all hosted on Microsoft Azure through our governance system. The Companion is an AI service that runs in the cloud, because the book is too large to run on a phone. In our apps, optional on-device AI runs on your device and sends nothing to us.

**5.5 Where data is stored versus where a model runs.** All data we store stays in the **United States.** For several models, Microsoft offers only "Global" deployments in the U.S. This means that, while a model reads your scrubbed question, that request can be processed in a Microsoft Azure region outside the United States. Microsoft's terms say that data at rest stays in the U.S. and that prompts are not used to train models. Separately, the Companion is offered only in the U.S. at launch.

**5.6 Microsoft's abuse monitoring.** Fluxline does not retain your questions or answers. Our AI provider, Microsoft Azure, applies automated abuse monitoring to its AI services. Under Microsoft's standard policy, Microsoft Azure keeps prompts and answers in temporary automated abuse-detection logs for up to 30 days, and content its systems flag as potentially abusive can be reviewed by authorized Microsoft personnel. We have not yet applied for or received Microsoft's "modified abuse monitoring" status, which would turn this off for our use.

**5.7 Human review of AI.** Two different things happen, and we keep them distinct. (a) When we use AI to help create our own content and services, a person reviews it before it is published or delivered. (b) When you use an AI feature, such as the Companion, you receive the answer directly, and a person does not review it first. Our Responsible AI Usage policy explains both.

**5.8 Reported answers.** If you tap "report" on an answer, we keep that question, that answer, and a reference ID for 30 days, encrypted, so we can review it. It is not linked to your account, so we cannot reply to you. After 30 days it is deleted automatically.

**5.9 Standards.** We align AI handling with the NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 (https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) and the NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0. These are frameworks we follow. They are not certifications.

## 6. What you turn on yourself

These are off until you choose them:

- **Reporting an answer** in the Companion (per answer).
- **Chat history in a Fluxline app.** If you turn it on, it stays on your device, is not sent to Fluxline, is excluded from cloud backups, is deleted after 30 days, and can be cleared at any time. Exchanges that triggered the crisis path are never saved. On the web, chat is session-only, with no history option.
- **Backups.** You choose where an encrypted backup goes (for example, your own iCloud, OneDrive, or Google Drive). We cannot open it. **If you lose your passphrase, no one can recover your data, including us.**
- **Optional cloud AI features in an app,** on a per-feature basis.
- **Research sharing,** if it is ever offered. It would be a separate opt-in with its own plain explanation. The Companion contributes no data to research.
- **Marketing email.** You can unsubscribe at any time.

## 10. Your choices and rights

**10.1 The four doors for deleting.** You can delete your data from the Companion in one step. You can also send a privacy request, delete your Fluxline account (which erases your Companion data automatically), or let your plan end (which deletes everything tied to it within 30 days).

**10.2 What deletion does.** We destroy the key that protects your data, so anything left cannot be linked back to you. Because our governance system never received an identifier, there is nothing to erase there. We send one confirmation email from your account address and then forget it. After you delete, you go through consent again before your next question.

**10.3 Your rights.** You may:

- **Access** what we hold about you. For the Companion that is your consent record, entitlement status, and usage counts. There is no question history to export, because none exists.
- **Correct** your details. Do this at account.fluxline.pro.
- **Delete** your information.
- **Export** your content. Our apps use an open Markdown backup.
- **Opt out** of marketing at any time.
- **Withdraw consent** for anything you opted into.
- **Ask how your data has been used.**

To make a request, write to privacy@fluxline.pro. We respond within 45 days.

**10.4 California protections.** Fluxline voluntarily gives all U.S. users the core California rights: to know, to delete, to correct, to opt out of any sale or sharing (we do neither), to limit use of sensitive information, and to be free of discrimination for using these rights.

**10.5 Visitors and customers outside the United States.** The RCF AI Companion, the RCF Founders Pass, the Identity in Motion program, and our apps are offered only in the United States at this time (see Terms of Use 9.6). Outside the United States, we offer only the digital editions of the book (the eBook, Audiobook, and Workbook PDF). If you bought one of the U.S.-only products above while living outside the United States, you may request a full refund by contacting support@fluxline.pro.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, Fluxline Resonance Group, LLC is responsible for your information. When you buy a digital edition or create an account, we collect your name and email address to deliver your purchase, give you access to your account, and provide support. On our websites, analytics and other non-essential cookies run only after you accept them in our cookie banner, and you can reject them at the first step. We rely on our contract with you, our legitimate interests in running and securing the Services, legal obligations, and your consent. You may ask to access, correct, delete, or export your information, object to its use, and complain to your local data protection authority. We keep your name and email while your account is open, and order records as stated in Section 9. We store information in the United States and rely on the transfer safeguards our providers offer, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 11. Security

We protect information with encryption in transit and at rest, access controls limited to the people and systems that need them, and reviews of our systems. Local app data uses AES-256 encryption (SQLCipher). We never store your passphrase. We do not put personal data in web addresses, and we keep personal identifiers out of standard application logs. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 15. If something goes wrong

If a security incident affects your information, we detect, contain, assess, and fix it, notify affected people directly, and publish what happened without defensive filler. By design, a breach of the Companion could expose metadata, counts, entitlement status, and reported answers inside their 30-day window. It could not expose a question history, because we do not keep one.
