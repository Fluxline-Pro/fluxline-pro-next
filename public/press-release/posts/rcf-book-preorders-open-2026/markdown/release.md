## What the book is

The Resonance Core Framework™ (RCF) is a practical system for making change that holds, built on alignment rather than force. Its premise is that sustainable change comes from resonance: alignment between your identity, values, feelings, and actions.

Until now, the framework has been delivered through Fluxline's coaching and consulting and explored on [The Resonant Identity podcast](https://theresonantidentity.com/start-here/). The podcast is where the framework is spoken. The book is where it is mapped.

The book runs 31 chapters in three parts: The Self, The Outward Reach, and Systemic Architecture & Legacy.

## What the framework covers

The framework's core concepts include:

- **Identity Coherence.** When your values, expression, and behavior agree. Not perfection, alignment.
- **Behavioral Gravity.** The pull a steady, regulated person has on the people around them.
- **The Window of Choice.** The space between a trigger and your response, where a conscious, identity-aligned choice becomes possible.
- **The DRIVE Model.** Six stages (Spark, Coherence, Activation, Momentum, Endurance, Embodiment) that carry a DRIVE (a repeatable, identity-aligned action) from first spark to lived identity.
- **The Decision Integrity Index (The DII).** A structured way to score how well a decision aligns with your identity, anchored to six core values: Embodied Awareness, Modular Precision, Resilient Alignment, Somatic Discipline, Creative Truth, and Strategic Innovation.

The full reference for these terms is on the [framework page](https://www.fluxline.pro/resonance-core-framework/).

## Who it is for

The book is for founders, leaders, creatives, small teams, and growing businesses who want clearer decisions and change that holds. The RCF is a decision and identity framework. It is not therapy.
